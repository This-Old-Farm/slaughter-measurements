Live station pages

Three versions to try on the floor. They show the same thing laid out differently. Once the operator picks one, the others are deleted (#62).

  • A - Next scan: the next barcode to scan in huge type, readable from across the room.
  • B - Checklist: the current animal's steps as a checklist beside today's animals.
  • C - Tiles: one colored tile per step, with today's animals below.
