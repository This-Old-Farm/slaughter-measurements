Live station

  1. Scan the animal ID (ends in B, P or L). The weight is captured on its own.
  2. Scan each step the screen asks for, in order: firearm, dirty (beef, lamb), +30 months and dairy (beef), then sex/type. Scan the "no" barcode too.
  3. Wrong scan? Scan the animal ID again and redo the steps. Both are kept, and the more complete one is used.
  4. "No valid weight"? Scan the animal ID again to reweigh.

Hang station

  1. Put the half carcass on the scale, then scan any unused hang tag.
  2. A bad weight can be fixed later: bring the carcass back and scan the same tag.

Trying the versions

The three versions show the same thing laid out differently. Switch between them and turn parts off to see what the operator actually uses. Once a version is picked, the others are deleted (#62).
